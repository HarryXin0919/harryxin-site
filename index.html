About关于

I’m an IB student taking HL Mathematics AA, Physics, and English B, building toward physics, math, or engineering at university — and one day, work that ships.我是个 IB 学生，HL 修数学 AA、物理与英语 B；大学想往物理、数学或工程走，日后做些真正能落地的东西。

Most of what I do starts as a question and ends as something you can hold or run: an electrochemistry experiment, a seating-optimization model, a storage gadget that lights up the part you’re looking for, or a small command-line tool. I also make literary & documentary videos — close readings, reflections, things worth slowing down for.我做的事多半始于一个问题，终成一件拿得到手、跑得起来的东西：一个电化学实验、一个座位优化模型、一个你找哪格、它就亮哪格的收纳盒，或是一个命令行小工具。我也拍文学与纪录片：细读一本书、做点反思——那种值得你慢下来看的东西。
