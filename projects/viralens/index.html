01 THE QUESTION

Which patterns remain after leaving one creator or one zone?离开单个创作者、单个分区后，哪些规律还站得住？

Creator advice often begins with one successful channel and stops there. I wanted a workflow that could repeat the same comparison across different creators, label reversals, and distinguish a useful observation from a universal claim.很多创作建议从一个成功账号出发，也停在一个账号。我想建立一套可以把同一比较重复到不同创作者、标记反例，并区分“值得观察”与“普遍规律”的工作流。

The report is allowed to say “inconclusive.”报告必须允许自己写下“无法下结论”。

02 METHOD

A repeatable path from API to verdict.从 API 到判断，过程可以复现。

  1. 01

    Collect获取

    Pull public video metadata from Bilibili and YouTube, then save clean CSV and JSON.获取 B 站与 YouTube 公开视频元数据，并保存为整洁的 CSV 与 JSON。

  2. 02

    Compare within creator创作者内比较

    Compare each creator with their own top and bottom reference videos before looking across zones.先在同一创作者内部比较头部与尾部参考视频，再跨分区观察方向。

  3. 03

    Stress-test扩大检验

    Repeat the same hypothesis across creators, platforms, and content zones; record counterexamples.把同一假设重复到不同创作者、平台与分区，并记录反例。

  4. 04

    Publish the verdict公开判断

    Mark each hypothesis supported, falsified, or inconclusive, with the underlying charts.把假设标为支持、推翻或无法判断，并附上对应图表。

03 OBSERVED ASSOCIATION

The most consistent separation appeared around each creator's signature form.最稳定的分离，出现在每位创作者自己的标志性形式附近。

Within the selected samples, the top-five and bottom-five groups were separated by 3.7×–109× on Bilibili. The English YouTube extension showed a 3.5×–8.8× separation across four creators.在所选样本中，B 站创作者头部 5 条与尾部 5 条之间出现 3.7×–109× 的差距；四位英文 YouTube 创作者的扩展样本中，差距为 3.5×–8.8×。

Boundary: this is an observed within-creator association. It does not establish that format produced the performance difference.边界：这只是创作者内部观察到的关联，不能证明视频形式产生了播放表现差异。

04 THE REVERSAL

The title-with-“you” hypothesis did not survive the wider sample.标题含“你”的假设，没有通过更宽样本的检验。

The first knowledge-zone comparisons looked favorable. After the same test expanded across zones and platforms, creator-level directions appeared on both sides and the geometric-mean ratio was 0.91×. I retracted the universal claim instead of hiding the reversal.最初在知识区的比较看起来偏正向；当同一测试扩展到更多分区和平台后，不同创作者的方向分布在基准线两侧，几何平均比值为 0.91×。我撤回了普遍性结论，而不是隐藏反转。

05 NEGATIVE RESULTS STAY

A clean report also shows what failed.可信的报告，也要展示没成立的假设。

ViraLens does not compress every chart into a success story. Its report vocabulary has three states:ViraLens 不会把每张图都包装成成功故事。报告只使用三种判断：

  • SUPPORTED — the tested pattern remained consistent in the stated sample — 在明确范围内方向保持一致
  • FALSIFIED — counterexamples broke the proposed generalization — 反例推翻了原先的普遍化说法
  • INCONCLUSIVE — the available data could not separate the alternatives — 现有数据不足以区分不同解释

06 LIMITS

What this study cannot claim.这项研究不能声称什么。

01

No causality没有因果结论

Public metadata is observational. Topic, timing, audience, distribution, and production quality can all vary together.公开元数据属于观察数据；选题、时间、受众、分发与制作质量可能同时变化。

02

Selected samples样本经过选择

Top-five and bottom-five comparisons are interpretable but small, and do not represent every upload or creator.头部 5 条与尾部 5 条容易理解，但样本很小，也不代表所有视频或所有创作者。

03

Missing private signals缺少私域指标

Public APIs do not expose every creator's impressions, click-through rate, or audience-retention curve.公开 API 不会提供所有创作者的曝光、点击率或观众留存曲线。

04

A map, not a recipe是地图，不是配方

The findings identify patterns worth investigating; they are not guaranteed recommendations for future videos.这些结果只标出值得继续调查的规律，不是对未来视频表现的保证。
